Drop your real images in THIS folder, then update the references in index.html.

Recommended files (names referenced in the HTML):
  hero.jpg       — your main hero photo (portrait, ~1000x1250px)
  about.jpg      — about-section photo (square, ~1000x1000px)
  og-image.jpg   — social share preview (landscape, 1200x630px)
  favicon.svg    — already here (the little browser-tab icon)

For the "My Content" gallery, add your video thumbnails / photos and
replace the placeholder tiles in index.html (look for <a class="tile" ...>).

Tip: keep images under ~300KB each for fast loading. Use https://squoosh.app
to compress without losing quality.
